## 1. Privacy at a glance

### General information

The following notes provide a simple overview of what happens to your personal
data when you visit this website. Personal data is any data with which you can
be personally identified. Detailed information can be found in the sections
below.

### Data collection on this website

**Who is responsible for data collection on this website?**
Data processing on this website is carried out by the website operator. You can
find their contact details in the "Information about the controller" section of
this privacy policy.

**How do we collect your data?**
Some of your data is collected because you provide it to us — for example when
you sign in with Twitch, create an event, submit a run, or upload an image.
Other data is collected automatically by our systems when you visit the site,
mainly technical access data (IP address, browser, time of the request). A
further part comes from your Twitch account when you sign in via Twitch.

**What do we use your data for?**
Part of the data is collected to ensure error-free and secure provision of the
website. Other data is needed to provide your sign-in, your participation in
events, and the display of scoreboards, calendar entries and overlays.

**What rights do you have regarding your data?**
You have the right at any time to receive information about the origin,
recipients and purpose of your stored personal data free of charge. You also
have the right to request the correction or deletion of this data, the
restriction of processing, and data portability, and you have a right to object.
You can contact us at any time regarding this and other questions on data
protection. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent
supervisory authority.

### No tracking, no advertising, no third-party CDNs

This website uses **no** analytics, tracking or advertising services. We do not
build behavioural profiles. Fonts, scripts, stylesheets and images are served
exclusively from this site's own server; nothing is loaded from third-party CDNs
(such as Google Fonts). Twitch and YouTube videos are not embedded — there are
only ordinary links, which connect to the respective provider only once you
click them.

## 4. Data collection on this website

### Cookies and local storage

This website uses strictly necessary cookies only. We use **no** marketing,
tracking or statistics cookies, which is why no cookie banner is displayed.

| Name | Purpose | Storage period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `oauth_state` | Protection against cross-site request forgery during sign-in with Twitch. Contains a signed random value, no user data. | 10 minutes |
| `refresh_token` | Keeps your session alive so you stay signed in. Contains a random value that is stored on the server only as a hash. | <30> days, or until you sign out |

Both cookies are `HttpOnly`, `Secure` and restricted to the sign-in path of the
API; they cannot be read by JavaScript in your browser. Your actual access token
is held only in your browser's memory and is not stored persistently.

In addition, we store the entry `soulsjwa.themeMode` in your browser's
`localStorage`. It only remembers whether you chose the light or dark
appearance. This value never leaves your browser and is not transmitted to us;
you can delete it at any time via your browser settings.

Legal basis: storing this information is strictly necessary to provide the
service you have expressly requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). The subsequent
processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR.

## 5. Sign-in, user account and allowlist

### Signing in with Twitch (OAuth 2.0)

On this website you can sign in only with an existing Twitch account. The
provider is Twitch Interactive, Inc., 350 Bush Street, 2nd Floor, San Francisco,
CA 94104, USA.

When you click "Sign in with Twitch" you are redirected to Twitch and asked
there whether you want to grant this website access to certain details of your
account. We request the `user:read:email` scope only. After you agree, Twitch
transmits the following details to us, which we store in our database:

- your **Twitch user ID** (the permanent numeric identifier of your account)
- your **Twitch login** (handle) and your **display name**
- the **email address** registered with your Twitch account
- the **URL of your Twitch profile picture**

In addition, we store the time your account was created and last updated, and
your role on this website (regular user or administrator). We never receive
passwords; the sign-in itself takes place entirely at Twitch.

The purpose of the processing is to recognise you, link your account to your
Twitch channel, contact you by email about changes to events, and assign you as
a competitor in an event. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of
the usage relationship) and the consent you give as part of the sign-in process
under Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR.

When the Twitch sign-in page is opened and the sign-in data is exchanged, data is
transferred to Twitch in the USA. Twitch is certified under the EU-US Data
Privacy Framework. For details on data processing by Twitch, see the Twitch
privacy notice: https://www.twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-notice/

You can revoke the permission you granted at any time in your Twitch settings
under "Connections".

### Allowlist (access approval)

Access to this website is restricted: signing in is only possible if your Twitch
login has previously been added to an allowlist by an administrator. We store
your Twitch login in lower case, an optional internal note on the reason for
adding it, the time it was added, and the administrator who created the entry.

If your login is not on the allowlist, the sign-in is aborted and no user account
is created. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR — we have a legitimate
interest in restricting access to a closed competition platform to invited
people and in preventing abuse.

### Session management

To keep your session alive we issue refresh tokens. In the database we store only
a cryptographic hash of them, a short prefix for lookup, the creation and expiry
times and the status (active/revoked). Each renewal invalidates the previous
token. When you sign out, your tokens are revoked.

## 6. Event, competition and content data

### Publicly visible competition data

This website exists to organise competitions in Souls games and to present their
progress publicly. If you take part in an event, the following data in particular
is visible to every visitor of the website, including visitors who are not signed
in:

- your display name, your Twitch login and your Twitch profile picture
- a link to your Twitch channel
- your placement, score and the objectives you completed per game
- timestamps and, where applicable, the times of your completions and scored runs
  (including runs marked as "trial")
- additional information about your run entered by you or the event
  organisers, such as comments and links to VODs and clips on Twitch or YouTube
- your planned runs in the event and site calendar (title, time, description)

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR: publishing this data is the purpose of
your participation in a public competition. Your email address is **not**
displayed publicly.

### Scoreboard overlays for streaming

Event organisers can create access tokens that allow a scoreboard to be embedded
as an overlay (e.g. in OBS). Anyone who knows the corresponding address can
retrieve the competition data it contains — which is public in any case —
without signing in. For each token we store a name, its creator, the creation
time and the revocation status.

### Uploaded images

Event organisers and administrators can upload images (e.g. event graphics).
Files are named and stored by their content; the original file name is not
retained, and embedded metadata (e.g. EXIF data with camera or location
information) is stripped on save. We store the checksum, image format,
dimensions, file size, the uploading user and the time. Do not upload images you
do not hold the rights to, or in which people are identifiable without their
consent.

### Audit log

Changes to events, objectives, competitors, roles, allowlists and legal documents
are logged. A log entry contains the type of change, the acting user, the
affected user where applicable, the time, an optional reason, and the state of
the changed data before and after the change. These entries are visible only to
administrators and — within their own event — to event organisers.

The purpose is the traceability of scoring decisions and protection against
manipulation. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Log entries are not
modified and are deleted after <24> months.

## 7. Desktop connector (optional additional software)

An optional Windows application ("connector") can be installed to record game
progress automatically. It is not required in order to use the website.

The connector reads values from the running game process on your own computer
(e.g. bosses defeated, play time, stat values) and transmits them to our server,
where they are evaluated against the event objectives. Only such game state data
and the identifiers of the event and game are transmitted. The connector
transmits **no** keystrokes, **no** screen contents, **no** files and **no**
other information about your computer.

Authentication uses an API key that you create in your profile. Of that key we
store only a hash, along with the name you gave it, a short prefix, the creation
time, an optional expiry time, the time it was last used, and the revocation
status. You can revoke keys in your profile at any time.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR; transmission only takes place while you
are actively using the connector.
